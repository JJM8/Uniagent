// why it matters

Three things it does differently.

🎙️

Voice-first

Speak and get answers. The watch talks directly to Uniagent — no phone, no app, just your voice and your AI.

🖨️

3D printed case

Custom shell, printed by you. Modify the design, choose your materials, make it yours. The STL files are open.

⚡

ESP32 powered

Reliable, well-known silicon with Wi-Fi built in. 16 MB of flash gives plenty of room for the firmware and voice processing.

Want to build one?

The Uniwatch is an open project — schematics, firmware, and case files are all available. Build it yourself or follow along.
