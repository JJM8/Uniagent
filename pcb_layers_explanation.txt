PCB Layer Explanation - Based on JLCPCB Email Query

1. Solder Paste Layer (typically shown in green):
   - Defines where solder paste should be applied
   - Slightly larger than the actual pad to ensure good solder coverage

2. Solder Mask Layer (typically shown in orange/red):
   - Protective coating over most of the PCB
   - Openings expose only the pads where components will be soldered
   - Usually slightly larger than the copper pad to ensure complete coverage

JLCPCB is asking which dimension to follow for your stencil - the paste layer opening or the solder mask opening, as they differ in size.
